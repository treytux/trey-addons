Website Sale Google Shopping

Google Shopping Feed for Odoo 16

Boost your sales by indexing your products on Google Shopping

Summary: Generates a compliant XML feed for Google Merchant Center, supporting product variants, taxonomy mapping, and multi-website configuration.

This module automatically generates a product feed from your Odoo e-commerce catalog. This feed is fully compatible with Google Merchant Center requirements, allowing you to showcase your products in Google Shopping search results efficiently.

Key Features

  • XML Feed Generation: Creates a dynamic /google-shopping.xml link.
  • Variant Support: Handles Size, Color, Material, and Pattern attributes correctly.
  • Taxonomy Import: Includes official Google Product Categories.
  • Advanced Mapping: Configurable fields for Gender, Age Group, and Condition.
  • Multi-Website: Specific settings per website.
  • Automatic Availability: Syncs stock status (In Stock/Out of Stock).

Configuration

1. Global Settings: Go to Website > Configuration > Websites. You will find a new "Google Shopping" tab to configure the feed cache, image size, and default shipping costs.

2. Product Categories: The module imports Google's taxonomy automatically. You can review it at Website > Configuration > eCommerce > Google product categories.

Product Setup & Field Mapping

Navigate to any Product Template and open the "Google Shopping" tab. Fill in the specific attributes to ensure your products are approved by Google.

Google Attribute Odoo Source Field Required?
g:id Product ID (+ Variant ID)
g:title Product Name (+ Variant Attributes)
g:description Product Sales Description
g:link Website Product URL
g:image_link Product Image (Resized per config)
g:availability Computed from Stock (Qty Available)
g:price Product Public Price
g:brand Product Brand (product_brand module)
g:google_product_category Google Shopping Tab > Category
g:condition Google Shopping Tab > Condition
g:gtin Barcode (EAN13) Conditional
g:mpn Google Shopping Tab > MPN Conditional

Generating the Feed

Your feed URL is:

http://your-odoo-domain.com/google-shopping.xml

Submit this URL to your Google Merchant Center account under "Products > Feeds" as a Scheduled Fetch.
