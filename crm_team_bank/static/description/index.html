CRM Team Bank

Assign bank accounts per sales team and show them on quotes and invoices

This module lets you configure the allowed bank accounts (res.partner.bank) per Sales Team. Users can then select one of those accounts on quotations and customer invoices, and the chosen account is printed on the corresponding PDF report.
