Website Sale Recommended Price

Show recommended price in website product page

This module adds the functionality to display the recommended price (RRP - Recommended Retail Price) on the website product pages and product listing cards. The recommended price is obtained from the product template and displayed alongside the standard sales price, helping customers understand the value proposition and any discounts applied.

Features

  • Display recommended price on product detail pages
  • Show RRP badge alongside the recommended price
  • Display recommended price in product listing cards
  • Multi-language support through translation files
  • Seamless integration with website_sale module
